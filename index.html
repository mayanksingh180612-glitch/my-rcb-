📚 CRICKET INFORMATION CENTER

🏏

Batting

Batting is the skill of scoring runs by hitting the ball. Common shots include drives, pulls, cuts and sweeps.

🎯

Bowling

Bowlers deliver the ball to dismiss batters and restrict runs. Main types include pace, swing, seam and spin.

🧤

Fielding

Fielders stop the ball, take catches and attempt run-outs. The wicketkeeper stands behind the stumps.

🏟️

Pitch

A cricket pitch is 22 yards (20.12 metres) long between the wickets.

🪵

Wickets

Each wicket has three stumps and two bails. A bowler aims to hit the stumps.

⚾

Cricket Ball

Cricket balls have a stitched seam. Red, white and pink balls are used in different formats.

IMPORTANT CRICKET RULES

1. Six runs: the ball crosses the boundary without bouncing.
2. Four runs: the ball reaches the boundary after bouncing.
3. Wide: an illegal delivery outside the batter's reach.
4. No-ball: an illegal delivery, with penalty runs.
5. Run-out: a batter is dismissed when a wicket is broken while outside the crease and the ball is in play.
6. Over: six valid deliveries in standard cricket.
